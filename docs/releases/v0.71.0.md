## Research rationale

Management Research Notes supports academic literature discovery and synthesis through concise, source-linked accounts of individual studies. Review improves this resource by making corrections traceable, not by claiming that any version is error-free. We publish completed corrections when their source evidence, independent review and version-specific checks are sufficient, while retaining unresolved work for further review. Withholding verified improvements until every note is perfect would preserve known errors without providing a corresponding evidential benefit.

This decision does not relax the treatment of factual errors. Material disagreements require source adjudication; corrected text requires fresh readers; unresolved repairs remain outside the released subset. Faithful compression and interpretive latitude can be accepted with explicit reasons. Reader agreement, numeric presence and mechanical PASS labels do not establish a corpus accuracy rate. Researchers should consult and cite the original papers for consequential claims.

## Method and tool changes

The [consolidated method](../analysis-workflow.md) introduces a compact private per-study source record before prose compression; ordered formula checks; explicit units, timing, comparator and scope checks; full-input reading and clause-tail coverage; separately attributed parent adjudication; finite closure on prescribed checks; and explicit selected/held release manifests. Canonical extraction, augmentation and claims instructions carry the relevant checks. The v3 note structure and rubric-v3 verdict definitions are retained.

The new [publication-evidence checker](../publication-readiness.md) checks a hash-bound parent record, current note/source/report provenance, both readers, pending corrections and residual dispositions. It cannot prove semantic accuracy or actual runtime identity; those remain review obligations. It was independently reviewed and hardened against stale embedded references and contradictory evidence records.

This release also includes the separately published October 5 numeric-tool correction: empirical year-range endpoints remain visible, ambiguous glyph candidates retain raw locations without adding hits, and explicit negative source values keep their sign. Its frozen 895-note sweep reproduces 46,916 numeric occurrences, 2,721 zero-hit occurrences across 455 notes, and 12,266 candidate locations. These are advisory diagnostic counts, not confirmed errors. No fitter, cap, parser, official report, source text or frontmatter-cleanup queue changed in this consolidation.
