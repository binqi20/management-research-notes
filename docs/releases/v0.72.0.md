## Scope and rationale

The complete first-pass source-fidelity review covered all 61 legacy V1 notes in NBS/2026-02. This release publishes minimal corrections to 40 eligible notes; 20 whole notes remain held and Spoor remains unchanged. It does not upgrade notes to V2/V3 or restart the paused AMJ retrospective wave. The aim is to remove verified errors while preserving supported analysis, historical provenance and independently recorded disagreements.

The selected changes affect 165 distinct fields: 58 exactly approved protected-frontmatter fields and 107 body fields. Final field grades are 86 substantive and 79 precision. Grades are parent judgments informed by independent source workshops; each field takes its highest corrected-clause severity. Substantive repairs correct a wrong attribution, number, unit, direction, formula or unsupported claim/prescription. Precision repairs retain a supported core while improving wording, coverage or qualification.

The application record contains 256 initial exact replacements across 163 fields, 27 later body replacements across 20 fields in 18 notes, and one final body replacement in Tabasi-Nejad. Repeated operations in one field do not create additional defective fields. Two later fields—Russell limitations and Skandera limitations—bring the distinct total to 165. Seven trailing spaces in six notes were normalized separately and are not substantive corrections.

The [complete correction table](v0.72.0-corrections.md) gives every changed clause with baseline/final wording, raw-source locations and rationale. Initial discoveries and later discoveries remain distinguishable.
