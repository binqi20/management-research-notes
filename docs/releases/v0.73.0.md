## Abstract handling and validation

The user authorized an optional PDF-derived abstract check for 17 extraction-layout conflicts. Local records bind paper identity, unchanged PDF/text paths and hashes, abstract bytes, a fixed extractor recipe and version, reproduced-text hash, and a visual review of the complete abstract boundaries. The validator reruns the extractor. It never overwrites the original PDF or text, accepts fuzzy wording, or treats a supplied transcript as evidence. Three book reviews—Acosta-Navas, Enderle and Reinecke—retain the Abstract heading with `Not reported in paper`.

This option changes only abstract validation. The standard fitted-source algorithm, note schema, anchor checks and claims quotation rules remain unchanged. Letter dehyphenation and ligature handling preserve numeric signs and ranges in the optional comparator; extraction itself can still lose typography, so reproducibility does not replace visual source review. Source-bearing evidence remains local. See [the method](../abstract-evidence.md).

## Evidence limits

The standard fitted input can omit source passages. Chen’s discussion supports the retained suppression interpretation outside the fitted block; Ghosh also retains raw-source dispositions for omitted context. In D’Amico, a right-column References marker removes raw lines 1218–1321, including left-column conclusion lines 1219–1244. The raw/PDF workshop checked that conclusion and the retained fitted passages; no reader was silently restamped as having seen omitted material, and the fitter was not changed in this correction batch.

Source-internal discrepancies remain disclosed rather than silently harmonized: D’Amico reports six green bonds in the main sample and five in a robustness discussion; Eaglin reports 100 versus 108 surveyed owners in different sections and inconsistent experience-direction wording; Ghosh’s main specifications describe center fixed effects while an endnote describes field-officer effects. Lokshin’s positive abstract summary is retained verbatim while the analytical note distinguishes a negative implementation estimate from an insignificant estimate under higher gender equality. Morris’s aggregate leverage interpretation does not mean every subgroup PE coefficient is null: Table 9H reports significant positive PE coefficients of 1.03 and 0.36 in the two largest-home strata. Its company-provided accounts include consolidated audited P&L accounts in the working sample.

V1 notes lack the empirical headings covered by the numeric scanner. Zero scanner tokens therefore provide no numeric clearance; study-specific numbers, units, comparisons and methods were checked manually against the source and tables. The workshop is a targeted verification of corrections and residuals, not a new blind exhaustive audit or a replication of the original studies.
