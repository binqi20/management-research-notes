# Being Scheherazade: The Importance of Storytelling in Academic Writing

**Abstract**
Not reported in paper

**Research Question**
How can narrative elements and the process of crafting a story enhance the impact of academic writing?

**Hypotheses / Propositions**
Not reported in paper

**Mechanism Process**
- Human face: portray actors, behavior, and human emotion through relatable anecdotes, examples, or rhetorical questions, while maintaining theoretical focus and avoiding domination by the research context.
- Motion and pacing: balance action that advances the story with commentary that explains and interprets it. Remove needless words, use active verbs, and vary sentence and paragraph length; neither rapid-fire reporting without interpretation nor excessive commentary serves the reader.
- Titles: capture attention and convey the article's essence economically. Popular-culture references can engage readers, but must relate to the article's main theme.
- First drafts: write consistently, break the work into small achievable assignments, and suspend self-criticism so the story's core elements can emerge.
- Feedback and revision: after polishing the first draft through major rewrites, seek specific feedback strategically from supportive readers, topic and publishing experts, nonspecialists, candid experts, and a copy editor. Listen and discuss instead of defending the writing.

The authors argue that storytelling helps readers engage with and understand academic ideas. Effective narrative design combines a recognizable human face with movement, explanation, and an informative title; crafting that narrative requires both protected drafting and subsequent criticism and revision.

**Theoretical Contribution**
The editorial brings insights from novelists and writing experts to academic storytelling, addressing both narrative elements and the process of crafting a manuscript. It argues that quantitative and qualitative researchers should devote as much attention to writing and storytelling as to identifying and answering interesting questions.
