# Research Methods Articles Designed to Advance Theory: An Update and Further Guidance

**Abstract**
Not reported in paper

**Research Question**
How can a research methods article contribute to theory development at AMJ, and how should authors frame methodological innovations that expand what management scholars can theorize about and test?

**Hypotheses / Propositions**
Not reported in paper

**Mechanism Process**
- Core distinction: improving evaluation of existing research questions versus enabling new research questions.
- Scope: quantitative and qualitative methods articles share the same overarching criteria for opening theoretical opportunities.
- Qualitative novelty: methods may be imported from other disciplines or adapted through bricolage to augment interpretation and generate new theoretical insights.

The editorial argues that methodological innovation can contribute to theory development by enabling new questions, study of previously inaccessible phenomena, new theoretical insights, or distinctions between competing explanations that were not previously possible or apparent to management scholars. Improving the execution of existing tests remains valuable, but this alone falls outside the intended AMJ methods niche. Even when a method is illustrated through one theory or context, authors should explain how other theoretical perspectives and contexts could benefit from the new questions or hypotheses it makes possible.

**Theoretical Contribution**
The editorial distinguishes making a theoretical contribution, the narrower expectation associated with substantive AMJ articles, from contributing to theory development, the broader aim of AMJ methods articles. It frames a methodological article's contribution through the change in what researchers know to be methodologically possible before and after the article. Novelty therefore resides less in methodological sophistication than in the new theoretical opportunities the method creates.

**Practical Implication**
Prospective AMJ methods authors should ask which new theoretical questions their method enables, which previously inaccessible phenomena it makes observable, and how it could challenge, extend, or prompt reconsideration of existing theories. Papers should be broad and accessible, provide example data and analysis scripts or code when possible, and use example research questions to clarify the method's possibilities; testing a substantive hypothesis is not required. The editors welcome quantitative submissions that introduce an innovative method and use a substantive question as the example hypothesis. Submissions whose main benefit concerns methodological suitability, method–epistemological alignment, efficiency, precision, or statistical performance may fit a methods-focused outlet better.

**Limitations**
The authors caution that many of their framing ideas represent a continuum and that the cited articles are exemplars rather than templates.

**Future Research**
Methods authors are encouraged to articulate a broader research agenda showing how their innovation could reshape theorizing across multiple domains, contexts, or theoretical perspectives. The authors call for as much novelty in specifying these future theoretical opportunities as in explaining the methodology itself.
