# Care-Full Reviewing at Academy of Management Journal: Celebrating Our Reviewers and Putting Care Front and Center in the Review Process

**Abstract**

Not reported in paper

**Research Question**

How does care-full reviewing manifest in practice at AMJ, and how can reviewers and associate editors navigate the tensions involved in evaluating and developing manuscripts?

**Hypotheses / Propositions**

Not reported in paper

**Mechanism Process**

- Key constructs: Regard, nurturance, and stewardship; care-full reviewing involves more than kindness or a developmental tone.
- Perfection versus good enough: Evaluate a manuscript on its own terms, prioritize load-bearing concerns, consider their potential solvability, and remain open to alternative approaches and necessary tradeoffs.
- Big picture versus detail: Adjust the granularity of feedback to the paper and its sections. General framing likely benefits more from a broader view than from granular evaluation, particularly early in the review process, while methodological problems that threaten validity need specific attention; consider how suggested methodological changes might alter findings and the proposed contribution.
- Judge versus advisor: Combine an orientation toward helping authors publish with clear explanations of barriers. Suggest potential pathways where possible, preserving authors' agency rather than prescribing the reviewer's preferred manuscript.

The editorial argues that care involves holding these tensions together through an investment in authors and their ideas. Reviewers should explain why problems matter and help identify viable paths for development, including when a manuscript ultimately appears elsewhere. This does not require abandoning judgment: when practical and timely solutions are elusive, or a fatal flaw in design, methods, or data makes a study unfit for purpose, reviewers should communicate that assessment forthrightly, respectfully, and humbly.

**Theoretical Contribution**

Building on the view that reviewing is caring, the editorial specifies care-full reviewing as regard, nurturance, and stewardship enacted in the review process. It offers reviewers and associate editors a shared vocabulary for navigating the three recurring tensions, clarifying how care extends beyond developmental tone to substantive evaluation and investment in authors.

**Practical Implication**

AMJ reviewers and associate editors should focus on fundamental concerns, explain what the problem is and why it matters for the specific manuscript, and engage with how it might be addressed. Clearly organized, numbered comments ordered by importance, together with respectful and candid editor-only comments, help editors synthesize feedback and communicate decisions. Offering more than one potential pathway can preserve authors' choices and clarify the issue; more detailed empirical matters can be addressed in later rounds after larger concerns have been addressed.
